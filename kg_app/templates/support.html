Frequently asked

What is Stafflynk?
Stafflynk is a field workforce and recovery management platform that helps organizations manage, track, and monitor their on-ground teams in real time with complete accountability.
Who can use Stafflynk?
Stafflynk is ideal for:
Recovery & collection agencies
NBFCs and banks
Field sales teams
Service and verification teams
Any organization managing field staff
What problems does Stafflynk solve?
Stafflynk removes confusion in field operations by providing:
Real-time staff tracking
Visit proof and reporting
Performance monitoring
Centralized control over field teams
Is my data safe and private?
Yes. Stafflynk ensures secure data handling with role-based access and protected cloud storage.
Can Stafflynk be customized for our organization?
Yes. Stafflynk can be configured based on your team size and operational needs.
